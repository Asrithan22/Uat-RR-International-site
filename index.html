Who We Are & What We Build

One Group.
Five Disciplines.

RR International Group brings engineering, mechanical, electrical, automation and instrumentation scope together under one accountable team, so a compressor package is engineered as a single system rather than assembled from separate ones.

01 The Group

RR International Group

A multi-discipline engineering group serving the oil and gas sector, with process gas compressor packages as its focus.

02 Discipline 01

Engineering & Design

Package engineering, layout and system design — from specification and datasheets through to detailed drawings and documentation.

03 Discipline 02

Mechanical

Skid and package mechanical scope — piping, structural frames, vessels, coolers, lube systems and rotating-equipment interfaces.

04 Discipline 03

Electrical & Power

Power distribution, motor and drive systems, cabling, earthing and hazardous-area electrical scope for packaged plant.

05 Discipline 04

Automation & Control

Control panels, PLC and HMI scope, unit control logic, safety interlocks and integration with plant control systems.

06 Discipline 05

Instrumentation

Field instrumentation, hook-ups, calibration, loop checking and the testing scope that proves a package before it ships.
